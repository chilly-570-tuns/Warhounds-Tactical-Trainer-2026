## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Warhounds**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assists for Warhounds single-player campaigns, including squad survivability, action-point control, ammunition management, medical support, and company finances. Use the toggles to reduce campaign friction while preserving the game's tactical structure, cover mechanics, ballistics, overwatch, classes, and mission objectives.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with squad builds, explore the campaign, reduce the impact of injuries and resource management, or replay missions with additional tactical freedom.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Squad Health** — Prevents friendly mercenaries from losing health during tactical missions.
- **Unlimited Tactical Equipment** — Provides unlimited use of supported mission equipment such as smoke, scanners, drones, and optical camouflage.
- **Instant Medical Recovery** — Reduces or removes recovery delays caused by injuries between missions.
- **Mission Resource Protection** — Protects supported mission resources from being consumed during tactical operations.

- **Unlimited Action Points** — Keeps the selected mercenary supplied with action points for movement, attacks, equipment, and positioning.
- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents equipped weapons from consuming ammunition during missions.
- **No Reload Requirement** — Allows supported weapons to fire without spending normal reload actions.
- **Guaranteed Accuracy** — Improves shot reliability while retaining the game's line-of-fire, cover, height, and flanking systems.

### 📌 Management

- **Unlimited Company Funds** — Prevents campaign finances from decreasing when purchasing equipment, recruiting fighters, or handling company expenses.
- **Unlimited Medical Supplies** — Keeps the medical-supply stock available for squad treatment and recovery.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Warhounds uses flexible action points for movement, attacks, equipment, and positioning. Activate Unlimited Action Points after entering a tactical mission, then move mercenaries freely while using cover, height, and flanking routes. Avoid ending the turn in exposed positions even when the trainer is active.

### Tools & Inventory

Equip mercenaries through the normal loadout and equipment screens. Unlimited Ammunition and Unlimited Tactical Equipment affect supported items during missions, while Unlimited Company Funds assists with purchasing gear and managing the roster between operations.

### World & Environment

The campaign takes place across an alternate-future East Africa in 2035. Between missions, manage the base, recruit mercenaries, upgrade facilities, handle logistics and finances, gather intelligence, select allies, and choose contracts. Save before major strategic decisions when using campaign resource options.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based and emphasizes real ballistics, destructible cover, vertical positioning, flanking, and cone-based overwatch. The five main classes are Assault, Specialist, Sniper, Machine Gunner, and Grenadier. Activate combat options only after tactical control is available, and deactivate them before loading another battle or returning to the strategic map.
